How It Works

Four steps to feeling
ready for anything

Designed to make practice more structured, more realistic, and more useful than anything you've tried before.

01

Choose a scenario

Pick from interviews, presentations, difficult conversations, and more. Set your difficulty and context.

02

Enter the environment

Step into an immersive space designed to feel real — so practice actually translates to performance.

03

Practise and reflect

Work through the scenario, test your responses, and get structured feedback on what to improve.

04

Improve over time

Track your growth across sessions. See what's clicking and double down on what needs work.

Features

Everything you need to
show up at your best

Built around what actually helps people perform — not just practise.

🎯

Immersive Practice Spaces

Step into realistic scenarios — interview rooms, presentation halls, tough conversations — designed for focused rehearsal.

💬

Structured Feedback

Review clarity, confidence, pacing, and delivery. Understand what you said, how you said it, and how to sharpen it.

🎚️

Adjustable Scenarios

Start easy, ramp up pressure. Build confidence gradually with scenarios that stretch you at the right pace.

📊

Progress Tracking

Repeat sessions, spot patterns, and see real development over time. You'll know exactly how far you've come.

🤝

Guided Practice

Solo rehearsal, peer feedback, or coached sessions — Performa flexes to your setting and learning style.

🌐

Flexible Access

Built with VR in mind, with a browser-based experience available during early development and testing.
